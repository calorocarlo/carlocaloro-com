Woyzeck / 2001

Woyzeck eludes traditional genre boundaries, being situated between performance and theater production. Using the means of today’s multi-medial world, Caloro stages a production that relies on digital imagery in addition to the presence of the actors. The script by Georg Büchner serves the artist as the in the play. Using various search engines on the Internet, in the key words or statements. These are projected in real time via a video beamer onto a table-high flat surface in the middle of the room. The artist’s own, freely of images from the Internet, interlinking them with the room. The drawings, similar to a rebus, remind us of the confused thoughts of the protagonist Woyeck and initially appear to be meaningless; but if we view them in they may be logically deciphered.
